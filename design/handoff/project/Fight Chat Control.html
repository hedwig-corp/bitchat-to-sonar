What is Chat Control?

A backdoor on every phone, by law

Chat Control (the EU’s CSA Regulation) would require messaging apps to run client-side scanning — software on your own device that checks your messages against a government-linked database and reports matches, before end-to-end encryption is ever applied. It doesn’t break the lock on the door. It puts a camera inside the room.

Scans before encryption

Your messages are read on your device before they’re sealed. "End-to-end encrypted" becomes meaningless the moment scanning is inserted at the source.

Scope creep is built in

A system that can match one kind of content can match any kind. The list of what’s reportable is a policy choice — one that can quietly expand.

It makes everyone less safe

A backdoor can’t tell friend from foe. The same weakness that lets a government scan lets criminals and hostile states in too.

What’s at stake

Who loses privacy first

Journalists & their sources — confidential tips become scannable and reportable.
Abuse survivors — the private channels they rely on to seek help are exposed.
Doctors & patients — medical conversations lose confidentiality by default.
Activists & dissidents — organizing becomes visible to whoever controls the list.
Every ordinary person — your family photos, run through a matcher that makes mistakes.
Businesses — trade secrets and legal privilege, scanned on the device.
