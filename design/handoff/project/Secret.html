Secret — interactive prototype

private assistant · Sonar encryption · Nostr sync · MCP tools

The idea

  • An AI chat app on the Sonar stack: no account, your Nostr key is your identity, history syncs E2E-encrypted over relays. Same DNA as the messenger, with a fuchsia brand accent.
  • Transport colors now mean where inference runs: cyan = on-device model, indigo = remote (your node, or paid models via Nostr DVM + Lightning).
  • MCP connectors with per-chat grants; sensitive tools (wallet) ask every time; web tools warn that the query leaves the device.
  • Skills are signed prompts/workflows — preinstalled, yours, or discovered on Nostr and zapped to their authors.

Try

  • Onboarding: continue as quietfox (your Sonar key) and choose where your AI runs — paid models are an explicit opt-in.
  • Open Plan my Thursday — tap a tool card to see args/result.
  • New chat (✦) → tap “Pay the router invoice” → wallet asks for approval with the amount.
  • Try “Anything new in the Marmot spec?” → web search warns before leaving the device.
  • Tap the chat header to switch models — bubbles change color to match; paid models append a sats receipt.
  • Wrench icon → per-chat tool grants. Settings → privacy dashboard, memory, sync.
