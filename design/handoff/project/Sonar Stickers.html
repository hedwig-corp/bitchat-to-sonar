Sonar Stickers

A community directory of sticker packs anyone can use in Sonar. Packs are published to the open Nostr network — no store, no gatekeeper. Find one you like and add it in two taps.

Made a pack? Publish it.

Sign your pack with your Nostr key and it shows up here automatically — you stay the owner, forever. No review queue, no fees.
